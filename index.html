A Warm Welcome

Situated in South Africa’s beautiful Franschhoek Valley, La Motte is home to the finest wines, recognised internationally for their exceptional quality. With its picturesque setting and historic charm, La Motte is an enchanting choice for those who appreciate authentic Cape Winelands hospitality.

Unique Estate Experiences

Explore wine, nature and art at La Motte Wine Estate, nestled in the heart of the Franschhoek Valley. From award-winning tastings and a scenic vineyard hiking trail to a gallery space celebrating South African art and design, this estate offers a rich blend of culture and countryside.

Food-Inspired Events

Experience the art of breadmaking at La Motte, where guests enjoy coffee and croissants, explore the estate’s historic water mill, learn traditional sourdough techniques, and savour a relaxed lunch before taking home their own freshly baked loaf, wine, and a special gift.

Art-inspired Events

Each exhibition has an exciting workshop schedule where guests can explore the creative process and make their own art in the Ateljee.

We have a wide range of memorable workshop experiences which include: Botanical papermaking, Textural acrylic painting, Ceramic Jewellery making, Plein air oil painting, Watercolour painting and Bookibinding.

Each experience is at your own pace while being surrounded by a relaxed and inspiring setting.
